# KT branding

The owner supplied and authorized `public/brands/kt-logo.png` for this website. The original PNG is 200 × 164 and is used unchanged by `components/telecom-brand.tsx`. Do not redraw, recolor, stretch or replace it with text. Display it at its intrinsic aspect ratio.

The header brand area contains only the KT image. The footer places a larger KT image next to a smaller SAmobile text wordmark at the lower-right foot of the logo, aligned near its bottom rather than vertically centered. Keep the marks close without overlap: red SA and dark mobile, no separate agency icon, separator or container. Tablet and phone layouts use their own spacing and sizes. The existing agency disclosure remains below this lockup.

Across all seven locales, text must use exactly `KT` and `SAmobile`. Never translate, transliterate, split, respell or change capitalization. This also applies to headings, metadata, accessibility labels and the language dialog.

SAmobile remains the sales agency in the utility bar, introduction, consultation and footer; it is not KT Corporation.
