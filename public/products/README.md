# Product image provenance

Downloaded 2026-09-22 for the internal SAmobile catalog demo. These are official manufacturer-hosted product images, retained as `reference-only`; public accessibility does not establish commercial republication rights. Obtain written approval or SAmobile-supplied assets before public release.

## Apple

Files under `apple/<product-slug>/<color>.png` were downloaded from `https://store.storeimages.cdn-apple.com/1/as-images.apple.com/is/` using the official Korean Apple Store `finish-select` asset identifiers at 940 × 1112 PNG. The corresponding Apple Korea product page is recorded in each `PhoneMedia.sourceUrl`.

## Samsung

Files under `samsung/<product-slug>/<color>.jpg` were downloaded from `https://images.samsung.com/kdp/goods/` URLs exposed in the official Korean Samsung product page structured data. They are 1600 × 864 product renders. The corresponding Samsung Korea product page is recorded in each `PhoneMedia.sourceUrl`.

Manufacturer-exclusive colors were not downloaded because they are not selectable in the SAmobile demo. Neutral SVG placeholders remain available as fallback assets.
